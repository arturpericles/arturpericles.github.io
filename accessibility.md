I want this website and its course materials to be usable by everyone,
including people who use assistive technology. The site is maintained with
the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as its technical
accessibility target.

## Report an accessibility problem

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please email
[amonteiro+accessibility@mc.edu](mailto:amonteiro+accessibility@mc.edu). Include the page or document,
the problem you encountered, and the assistive technology or browser you were
using, if applicable. I will make reasonable efforts to provide the material
in an accessible form and correct the underlying issue.

## Scope

This statement covers the pages and course materials published at
arturpericles.art. Some links lead to third-party websites or documents that I
do not control; accessibility concerns about linked material are still welcome
so that I can offer an alternative when possible.
